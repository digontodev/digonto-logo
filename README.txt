Digonto Logo 1.0
================
Make a complete logo and brand in four steps.
1 Brief     type the business name, tagline, what you do, style and colors
2 Ideas     dozens of different logo ideas appear: symbol, initials, wordmark, emblem, stamp.
            Press the heart to keep one, "more" for other takes on it, "More ideas" for a new set.
3 Customize change the name, symbol (1,494 icons, original shapes, initials, or your own SVG/PNG/JPG), layout (symbol left/top, two lines,
            wordmark, emblem, stamp, name badge, framed), colors and fonts. Two-tone symbols, background picture, undo/redo.
4 Export    a complete brand kit in one folder: logo files (SVG, PNG, PDF, EPS, AI) in 8 versions, favicon and app icons
            (ICO, ICNS, PNG), social images, and a brand guide PDF with colors, fonts, clear space and don'ts.
File > Save project keeps your work as a .dlgo file (older .ptlogo files still open).

Everything is exported as outlines, so nobody needs your fonts. Always check EPS/AI in your print software.
Licences: icons Phosphor Icons (MIT), fonts open source (SIL OFL / Apache 2.0). See logokit/data for the texts.
Only PySide6-Essentials is installed, locked with SHA-256 hashes.

Your own symbol: an SVG keeps its shapes (and colors if you like). A PNG/JPG is traced into one clean vector shape in your main color.
Background > My picture ... shows the logo on your own photo; the kit then also contains logo-on-my-picture.png.
